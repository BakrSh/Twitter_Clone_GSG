Everest Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Everest handles information when you connect your Google account to Everest for backup and recovery purposes.

1. Information We Access

When you connect Google Drive to Everest, the application may request access to your Google account information and Google Drive through Google's OAuth authorization system.

The application requests only the permissions necessary for its backup functionality, including the ability to manage files created or used by Everest in Google Drive.

2. How We Use Information

Information obtained through Google OAuth is used only to:

  • Identify the Google account connected to the backup destination.
  • Authenticate Everest with Google Drive.
  • Create and upload backup files to the configured Google Drive destination.
  • Maintain the connection so scheduled backups can continue.
  • Detect and recover from authentication or backup failures.

3. Google Drive Data

Everest does not use Google Drive data for advertising, profiling, selling information, or unrelated purposes.

Backup files uploaded by Everest are stored in the Google Drive account selected by the authorized user. The Google account owner controls access to those files through Google Drive.

4. OAuth Credentials

OAuth credentials required to maintain the Google Drive connection are handled by the Everest application using its server-side security mechanisms.

OAuth credentials are not intentionally shared with third parties except as necessary to communicate with Google services as authorized by the user.

5. Data Sharing

Everest does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information obtained through Google services is used only for the functionality described in this policy and is not shared with third parties for unrelated purposes.

6. Data Retention

Information associated with a Google Drive backup destination is retained for as long as the destination is configured and required by the Everest backup system.

The authorized user can disconnect the Google Drive destination through the application's backup configuration. Google account permissions can also be revoked through the user's Google account settings.

7. Security

Everest is designed to protect backup credentials and application data using appropriate technical and organizational safeguards.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

Everest uses Google APIs and Google Drive to provide authorized backup functionality. Use of Google services is also subject to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Everest application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
